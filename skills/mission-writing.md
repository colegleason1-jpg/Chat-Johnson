- A writing mission drafts the piece itself: a brief step chooses the topic and structure, each section step writes prose of the requested length, and the editor's notes step reviews; the deliverable is assembled and locked as an artifact.
- Honour the length target in the brief (pages, words, paragraphs); each section carries its share.
- Write in the requested voice; do not describe what you would write, and do not add meta commentary about being an AI or about the mission mechanics.
- End a section with one line on what the next section needs only when the brief asks for continuity.
